# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML5 + Tailwind CSS (no build framework). Matches the brief's own preferred deliverable ("HTML5 con clases de Tailwind CSS... lista para previsualizar"); a single marketing/landing site doesn't need app tooling, and static output keeps preview and handoff trivial.

## Users

Two primary audiences:
1. **Local consumers in Peru** deciding where to get soft-serve ice cream — drawn in by the 9 unique flavors, want a fun/photogenic experience, need to find a location or order delivery.
2. **Prospective franchise investors** evaluating Ice Pop Perú as a business opportunity — want to see brand positioning and profitability signals, and submit contact info to be followed up.

## Product Purpose

A commercial/marketing website for Ice Pop Perú, a Peruvian soft-serve ice cream chain, built to drive in-store/delivery sales and capture franchise investor leads. Commissioned as a professional deliverable valued at S/ 1,200 PEN.

## Positioning

Breaks from traditional soft-serve (vanilla/strawberry) with 9 exclusive, visually striking flavor combinations and premium toppings, deliberately designed to be photogenic and shareable on Instagram/TikTok — a neighboring ice cream shop selling standard flavors could not truthfully claim the same variety or social-media-native format.

## Operating Context

- Physical retail locations in Peru, plus delivery.
- Customer acquisition happens primarily through social media (Instagram, TikTok).
- Franchise sales run through a lead-capture funnel on the site.
- Peruvian consumer-protection law requires a "Libro de Reclamaciones" to be present.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Required sections: fixed header/nav, hero, "9 Sabores" showcase (grid/carousel), "La Experiencia Ice Pop" differentiation block, social proof/social feed module, franchise section with a lead form (Name, Phone, City, Investment capital), locations/map, footer.
- Legal: footer must include "Libro de Reclamaciones" and the legal entity name.
- Legal name (razón social) differs from the commercial/brand name — both must appear correctly in the appropriate places (commercial name in brand-facing UI, legal name in footer/legal copy).

## Brand Commitments

- Commercial name: **Ice Pop Perú**. Legal entity: **Dulces y Sabores del Perú S.R.L.**
- Social channels: Instagram @ice_pop_peru, Instagram franchise @icepop_franquicia, TikTok @icepopperu, LinkedIn "Dulces y Sabores del Perú S.R.L."
- The user gave a detailed visual direction (vibrant pastel/neon palette, rounded playful sans-serif type, rounded-2xl/3xl shapes, microinteractions). Noted as a binding constraint from the brief; full palette/typography/component decisions belong in DESIGN.md, produced during visual/new-work, not expanded here.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real, user-provided social handles (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn) for reference and for the social-proof module.
- Real logo file (`assets/logo.png`) — colors and typography sampled from it, not approximated.
- Real video: `assets/hero-video.mp4` (soft-serve cone being made, hero background) and `assets/experiencia-video.mp4` (toppings flying onto a cone, La Experiencia background).
- No product photography beyond the hero video, pricing, franchise economics, testimonials, or real store addresses were supplied. Future work must not fabricate specific financial claims, ROI figures, customer testimonials, or addresses — use placeholders and label them as such.

## Product Principles

1. Every section should drive one of two conversions: a store/delivery visit, or a franchise lead.
2. Visual identity must feel shareable — content and layout should look good in a screenshot.
3. Content must respect Peruvian consumer-protection requirements (Libro de Reclamaciones, correct legal entity name).
4. Tone shifts by audience: playful and tempting for consumer-facing sections, professional and investor-oriented for the franchise section.
5. Never invent specific business claims (revenue, ROI, testimonials) that weren't supplied — use clearly-marked placeholders instead.
